All bookings are subject to availability and are only confirmed once a deposit has been received. An unconfirmed booking does not guarantee a time slot.

  • A booking confirmation will be sent via email or message once the deposit is processed.
  • It is the client's responsibility to ensure all vehicle and contact details provided at the time of booking are accurate.
  • We reserve the right to refuse or cancel any booking at our discretion, in which case any deposit paid will be refunded in full.
02 Deposits & Payment Terms

All appointments require a non-refundable booking fee to lock in your time slot. This fee is credited directly toward your total balance due upon completion of the service — it is not an additional charge.

  • The remaining balance is due on the day of service, prior to or upon completion of the detail.
  • Accepted payment methods: cash, e-transfer, credit/debit.
  • Prices quoted are based on the vehicle condition described at the time of booking. If the vehicle is found to be in significantly worse condition upon arrival, we reserve the right to adjust the price or decline service. You will be notified before any work begins.
  • Full payment must be completed before the vehicle is returned to the client.
03 Cancellations & Refunds

We understand that plans change. The following policy applies to all cancellations:

Client-Initiated Cancellations
  • Cancellations made at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment: your appointment will be cancelled and your booking fee refunded minus a small admin fee to cover processing.
  • Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice: the booking fee is forfeited in full as a cancellation fee. A new booking fee will be required to reschedule.
  • No-shows (client or vehicle unavailable at the time of service without prior notice): the appointment is cancelled, the booking fee is forfeited, and a new booking fee will be required to rebook.
Service-Initiated Cancellations
  • If we are required to cancel due to unforeseen circumstances (illness, equipment failure, severe weather, etc.), you will receive a full refund of your booking fee or the option to reschedule at no penalty.
04 Rescheduling
  • One complimentary reschedule is permitted provided at least 24 hours' notice is given.
  • Rescheduling requests made with less than 24 hours' notice may result in forfeiture of the deposit.
  • A second reschedule request may be treated as a cancellation, subject to the cancellation policy above.
  • Rescheduled appointments are subject to availability.

  • Clients must disclose any known pre-existing damage, fragile parts, aftermarket modifications, or sensitive surfaces at the time of booking. Failure to do so may limit the scope of our liability.
  • We reserve the right to photograph the vehicle before and after service for quality control and liability documentation.
06 Liability & Damage Policy

We take great care with every vehicle and use professional-grade products and equipment. However, the following terms apply:

Pre-Existing Damage
  • We are not liable for any pre-existing damage, including but not limited to: scratches, swirl marks, chips, faded paint, rust, worn trim, cracked glass, or deteriorated seals.
  • Pre-existing damage will be noted at the start of the appointment. Clients are encouraged to review and acknowledge this documentation before work begins.
Chemical & Water Damage
  • Some surfaces (e.g., heavily oxidized paint, compromised clear coat, aging rubber seals) may react unexpectedly to cleaning products. We will exercise reasonable care, but cannot guarantee results on surfaces already in poor condition.
  • We are not liable for water ingress through pre-existing gaps, faulty seals, or open sunroofs/windows left by the client.
Mechanical & Electrical
  • We are not responsible for any pre-existing mechanical or electrical issues that may surface during or after the detailing process (e.g., malfunctioning windows, sensors, or electronics).
Claims
  • Any damage claims must be reported before the vehicle leaves the service location. Claims submitted after the vehicle has been driven away may not be accepted.
  • We will work in good faith to investigate and resolve any legitimate damage claims promptly.
